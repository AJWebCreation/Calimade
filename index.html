1. Standalone Offline Mode

This HTML training assistant is designed to accompany you directly in local mode or when you travel. It utilizes your local storage cache to securely preserve physical statistics, customized journals, training milestones, and athletic profiles.

2. What is an EMOM Workout?

EMOM stands for "Every Minute On the Minute". It is a highly systemic style of intervals. Upon launching the circuits clock, you are tasked with completing a set repeat workload within each 60-second frame. All remaining time inside that minute serves as your resting window before the subsequent round commences.

3. Success Advice for Beginners

Keep your compound progressions humble. Target total body stability and full range of motion. Use the Workouts -> Academy tab to read comprehensive mechanics of Statics, Isolations, and Cardio.
